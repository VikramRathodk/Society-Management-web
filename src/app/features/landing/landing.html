Transparency

Nothing about money changes quietly.

Whoever records an entry, someone else approves it. Every change to accounts, entries and settings is written to the audit log with who did it and when. Close a period with a lock date and it stays closed.

  1. add_circle Ramesh K. recorded an expense: Lift maintenance ₹12,000 18 Nov · 09:12
  2. task_alt Sunita M. approved it 18 Nov · 11:40
  3. lock Sunita M. locked the books up to 31 Oct 19 Nov · 18:05
